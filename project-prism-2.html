Prism 2

Under Development Unreal Engine 2026

Overview

Prism 2 is an Unreal Engine puzzle game currently under development. The project focuses on shooting puzzles and moving or rotating platform interactions, challenging players to aim carefully, understand each mechanism, and find a path forward. Everything is created in Unreal Engine, from the gameplay systems and level construction to the interactive presentation.

My Responsibilities

  • Developing the game entirely within Unreal Engine
  • Building shooting-based puzzle interactions for the player experience
  • Creating moving and rotating platforms that change how players navigate each puzzle
  • Creating gameplay systems, player interactions, and reusable components
  • Designing levels around spatial reasoning, timing, movement, and exploration
  • Iterating on puzzle clarity, responsiveness, and overall game feel

Technical Highlights

  • Gameplay: Unreal Engine systems for player control, interaction, and progression
  • Shooting Puzzles: Aim-based interactions that let players activate, alter, or solve parts of the environment
  • Platform Mechanics: Moving and rotating platforms that create changing routes and timing challenges
  • World Building: Unreal-based level construction, environmental layout, and puzzle design
  • Iteration: Rapidly testing and refining features while the project remains under development

Featured Components

Shooting Puzzles

Shooting is used as a puzzle-solving tool, letting players interact with mechanisms and think carefully about timing, position, and aim.

Unreal Gameplay Foundation

Moving and Rotating Platforms

Platforms move and rotate to change the available routes, creating spatial logic and timing challenges as players work through each level.
